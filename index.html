Jon Gudjonsson

Financial Economics and Macroprudential Policy

Central Bank of Iceland · Copenhagen Business School

I am an economist in the Financial Stability department of the Central Bank of Iceland, where I work on macroprudential policy analysis and applied research on household credit and mortgage markets. I am also completing a PhD in economics at Copenhagen Business School (expected 2026), as a PhD Fellow at PeRCent, the Pension Research Centre.

My research is in financial economics and macroprudential policy, with secondary interests in computational economics, macroeconomics, and pension and household finance.
