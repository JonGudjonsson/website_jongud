Macroeconomics, Finance and Financial Stability

Central Bank of Iceland · Copenhagen Business School

I am an economist at the Financial Stability Division of the Central Bank of Iceland, currently also completing a PhD in economics at Copenhagen Business School.

My interests are in macroeconomics, financial economics, and financial stability, and my dissertation research looks at the impact of pension fund investment on financial stability.
