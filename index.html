Jón Guðjónsson

Economist — Macroeconomics, Finance & Financial Stability

I am an economist working at the intersection of macroeconomics, financial economics, and financial stability. My research examines how financial conditions shape aggregate dynamics, with particular interest in small open economies and macroprudential policy.
