About this model

This is a simplified IS-LM model. The IS curve represents goods market equilibrium; the LM curve represents money market equilibrium. The intersection gives the short-run equilibrium output and interest rate.

Note: This is a pedagogical illustration, not a full calibrated model. Parameters are stylised.
